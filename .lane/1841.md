# Lane #1841 — the Wallet submenu's third row: the balance

dig_ecosystem#1841 asked for three things the Wallet submenu could honestly deliver today. Two
already shipped:

* **the receive address, copyable** — #52, gated on its real precondition and naming its own reason
* **an explainer naming what is not built yet** — `My wallet…`, from #45, which also put `Wallet`
  on the spine between Manage Account and Security

The third was missing: **the balance, or an honest line saying why it is not known**. It existed
only inside the wallet window, one click away — so the submenu could not answer "what do I hold?",
and with no account it opened onto a lone `My wallet…` a person had to click to learn there was
nothing to show.

## What this lane adds

One always-present, always-enabled row carrying the reading as its label, opening the window that
holds the full reason. That is the shape **Cache** already uses for its disconnected state, and the
row shares `AboutWallet` with the explainer for the same reason Cache's two rows share `AboutCache`:
the window is genuinely the same one.

No spending verb. The money path stays parked (#1702) and no `TrayAction` can spend, so "the tray
cannot move funds" remains structural rather than one `enabled: false` away from being wrong.

## The defect the tests caught

`WalletOverview::of_tray` collapsed three account states into `Locked`, so a host with no credential
store, an account with no password yet, and an account whose unlock is *what already failed* were all
told to **"unlock"**. `AddressUnavailable` now carries one variant per REMEDY; exhaustiveness then
forced the same fix through the window's address and balance prose.
